## Scope

Keep dashboard (home), plus the app shell it sits in. Readiness and Road inherit this world. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

One person on a phone, before training and at the morning and evening watches, often in low light. Job: see the chapter and countdown, check readiness, read today's main order, begin the mission. Constraints: offline, large touch targets, readiness never conveyed by color alone, no scores or streaks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Keep is a trail-dawn mission board. One short forest band holds everything about the chapter, so readiness and today's main order own the phone screen. It refuses both the stats-card grid and a scenic hero that pushes the orders below the fold.

OWN-WORLD: A deep forest band over a painted misty dawn plate; warm paper page; parchment medallions with line icons; Hearth Gold only on the pine mark, theme line, main-order top edge and selected tab. Wittgenstein serif for names, headings and numerals; Noto Sans for everything read or tapped. 1px borders, small radii, no shadows. Dark variant follows the phone, drawn from the Night Atlas palette.

STORY: The visitor sees where they are in Chapter I, checks readiness first, reads the main order, sees both watches and the Hearth mission, and begins.
