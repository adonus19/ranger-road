Use case: ui-mockup. High-fidelity mobile app UI design comp. Flat frontal screenshot of one phone screen at 390x844 viewport proportion.

Surface: The Ranger's Road, "Log a walk": a private, offline training app screen for recording a walk the person has just finished. The visitor's job: fill one short form in two parts and save. It must read instantly as a working app form.

Reference image: it carries the visual world to keep exactly: header bar, warm paper page, serif plus sans pairing, small spaced-caps field labels, bordered tap-scale buttons, pine button, bottom tab bar. Do NOT copy the reference's content (no readiness text, no energy or sleep fields); this screen's form is described below.

Layout, top to bottom:
1. Header bar as in the reference.
2. A text link with a left arrow: "Back to Road".
3. Large serif page title "Log a walk".
4. Serif section heading "The walk".
5. Label "DATE", then three bordered segmented choices in one row: "Today" (selected, pine fill, cream text), "Yesterday", "Pick a date".
6. Two fields side by side, equal width: label "MILES" over a bordered field showing the serif numeral "2.1"; label "MINUTES" over a bordered field showing the serif numeral "41". Numerals large, as the visual weight of this part.
7. Label "TERRAIN", then a full-width bordered text field containing "Gravel trail".
8. A thin rule, then serif section heading "How it felt".
9. Label "EFFORT (RPE)", then a tap scale of ten bordered square buttons numbered 1 to 10 in two rows of five, serif numerals, "6" selected with pine fill and cream numeral. Help line: "1 is very easy. 10 is as hard as you can go."
10. A quiet collapsed row with a plus sign: "Add pain before and after (optional)".
11. Docked at the bottom, just above the tab bar, a full-width pine button "Save walk", with a thin paper strip behind it so it reads as docked over the scrolling form.
12. Tab bar.

Text, verbatim and correctly spelled: "The Ranger's Road"; "Back to Road"; "Log a walk"; "The walk"; "DATE"; "Today"; "Yesterday"; "Pick a date"; "MILES"; "2.1"; "MINUTES"; "41"; "TERRAIN"; "Gravel trail"; "How it felt"; "EFFORT (RPE)"; "1" through "10"; "1 is very easy. 10 is as hard as you can go."; "Add pain before and after (optional)"; "Save walk"; "Keep"; "Road"; "Forge"; "Journal"; "Field Manual".
Palette only: Night Forest #172B21 for the header bar, Pine #24362B for primary buttons and selected scale options, warm paper #F6F3EA page, Parchment Tint #E9E5D8 for quiet notices, rules #D4D1C7, Gold Line #A9804A only as the selected tab's short underline, Ink #171A17, Secondary Ink #4F514D. Flat surfaces with 1px borders and 6px corners, no shadows.
Type: a sturdy bracketed book serif for the page title, questions, section headings and all numerals; a narrow humanist sans for labels, help lines, inputs and buttons. Field labels are small spaced capitals in sage gray, like the reference. No decorative fonts.
Top of the screen: the same dark forest header bar as the reference, with the small gold three-pine mark and "The Ranger's Road" in cream serif. Bottom: the same five-tab bar as the reference, "Keep", "Road", "Forge", "Journal", "Field Manual", with "Road" selected (pine, short gold underline).
Avoid: maps, route lines, running figures, invented goals or targets, pace, XP, points, streaks, badges, charts, photos, forest scenery, fake parchment texture, glow, 3D, device frame, status bar, hands.
