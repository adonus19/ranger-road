# Active Gate Trial — Station extension

The approved Station preparation composition remains the visual authority. [The active circuit comp](gate-trial-active-station-v2.png) shows one illustrative saved entry in Round 1. Its `6 min` rest and `10` completed reps are sample user data, not defaults. The **Box squat** heading leads without an eyebrow.

## Layout at 390 × 844

- Keep the existing forest painting, wordmark, title, paper surface, five-stop route, rules, type, and fixed Road tab bar. Compress the forest band during recording to about 155–175 px so the current task and primary action fit on a phone. Do not turn each stage into a card.
- The five route stops represent Road, Gate Circuit, Mind, Spirit, and Oath. A completed stop gets a filled mark; the active stop gets the gold ring; future stops remain outlines. Expose names and current/complete state to assistive technology.
- Use one large serif heading for the active phase. Pair it with a plain subline for the round or task. Place the next required action close to the relevant input; fix the primary Save/Continue action above the tab bar.
- In the circuit, show one movement at a time. Keep its exact target and a preview of its existing image sequence visible. **Quick Help** opens the full instructional text and sequence without leaving the active round. A 48 px **Record pain** action opens immediate pain capture; a separate 48 px **Stop trial** action saves the partial draft and stops physical work. Do not make a pain report depend on a later Save tap.
- The active movement entry captures actual reps or seconds, including left/right where prescribed. Do not prefill completed values from targets. The comp's entered `10` is illustrative. Every tap target is at least 48 px. Use native number keyboards and visible field labels.
- Box Squat's sequence may show the model's face; the neck-down rule applies to **muscle maps**. Use the approved exercise assets already in `public/images/exercises/`, rather than regenerating them for the trial.

## Five-phase record

| Stop | Main task and fields | Advance rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Road | Brisk 2-mile walk. Enter time, RPE, knee response, back response, and recovery after 5 minutes. Average heart rate is optional. | Save the walk data before opening Gate Circuit. |
| Gate Circuit | Record actual rest after the walk, then three controlled rounds of: 10 box squats, 8 push-ups, 5 assisted pull-ups, 8 step-ups per leg, 30 sec suitcase carry per side, 20 sec side plank per side. Record each station's actual result and total completion time. | At least 5 minutes of rest; longer than 10 remains allowed for safety. Do not race. A stopped or reduced circuit remains a partial record. |
| Mind | Three separate written responses: one thing learned about body, character, and family. | Save each response as the person writes it. |
| Spirit | Link to Psalm 121 by reference, confirmation of reading and prayer, a quiet prayer timer/actual duration, and the documented husband/father question. | At least 10 quiet minutes; longer than 15 remains allowed. |
| Oath | A short personal Ranger's Oath, with the documented themes as guidance. | Review the five parts, then explicitly record completion. |

## States and persistence

- Preparation requires a same-day **Green · Ready** check before the full physical trial. **Yellow · Reduce** and **Red · Restore** defer completion; keep the partial draft and the person's entries. Show the words, not color alone.
- A pain event at severity 5 or higher ends strength work in the circuit. Keep the event and partial entries; do not advance to another physical station or represent the effort as a completed Gate Trial.
- Resume opens the saved phase, round, and movement. Back navigation retains draft data. The route must never imply a phase was completed just because a screen was opened. Pain or a decision to stop must preserve all prior phase values and the pain event in local history.
- Show a review before final save, then a permanent completion summary. The recovery function assessment is a separate follow-up 60–120 minutes after the effort; do not require it to complete the trial.
- Do not add pace targets, speed scoring, badges, invented exercises, faith points, or chapter unlocks. Keep the trial available offline.
