Winter stand-in

You can choose the stand-in on any day, with an optional weather reason. It is one continuous walk of 1.5 to 2 hours. No treadmill: take gain from local hills or stairs outdoors when you can, otherwise an indoor track. Gain is recorded, not required.

Navigate on paper with a real trail map: route, junctions, distance, gain and turnaround. Estimate your location at set checkpoints. The 5 quiet minutes and the reflection come at the halfway mark.

Weather and turning back

If the weather turns, or you turn back for daylight, time, a lost trail or another turnaround reason, mark the hike “Postponed”. A postponement is not a failed attempt; try again another day. Turning back for pain ends that attempt, kept in your history like any trial.
