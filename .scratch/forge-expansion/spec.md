Approved 2026-10-16 after a grilling session. Tickets are in `issues/`. Each ticket is one session: run tests and the build, deploy, then stop with a line in the hand-off log at the bottom of this file.

**Deadline:** tickets 04, 05 and 06 (and image batch 10) are needed before Chapter II starts, around Monday 2026-11-02 (the Gate Trial). Chapter I is live; its Forge A and B do not change.

## Equipment

The person has: a barbell and rack, an adjustable bench (upright and incline), dumbbells, bands, a pull-up bar with clearance above it, a dip station or parallel bars, and clear wall space. Bands anchor on the pull-up bar (high) or a rack upright (chest height). There is no separate chest-height anchor.

### Press stance

- Chapters I–II: always seated.
- Chapter III: transition. Seated by default; half-kneeling (one-arm) offered.
- Chapter IV on: if today's readiness has **back pain ≥ 2**, the press is done seated or half-kneeling (the person chooses). Barbell: seated barbell with a "reduce the load" note, or a swap to dumbbells at the person's choice. Otherwise standing is allowed.

### Curls

Forge A: hammer curl every chapter. Forge B: DB curl in II, IV, VI, VIII; preacher curl in III, V, VII, IX.

### Overhead carry progression

- Ch I–II: two-hand, light, 20–30 sec
- III–IV: 30–45 sec, heavier
- V–VI: 45–60 sec
- VII–VIII: waiter carry joins as the harder step
- IX: maintenance

In each session the press comes before the carries, and the overhead carry is the last carry.

### Balance (later chapters)

From Chapter V, in Forge B's tenth slot when it is not the glute bridge: single-leg balance with harder versions (eyes closed, head turns, soft pad), slow lateral step-down, single-leg reach (three directions), light single-leg Romanian deadlift gated by back pain like the light hinge.

## Skill tracks

A track is one slot whose exercise steps up over the campaign. Each step is its own exercise in the library.

| Track | Slot | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| Pull → muscle-up | Forge A vertical pull | assisted pull-up → band-light pull-up + slow negatives → bodyweight pull-up → explosive / chest-to-bar pull-up → straight-bar dip + transition drills → band-assisted muscle-up → muscle-up |
| Push → wall handstand push-up | Forge B push-up | push-up → pike push-up → feet-elevated pike push-up → wall handstand-push-up negatives → wall handstand push-up (freestanding is a stretch goal) |
| Handstand balance | Forge A slot 10, from Chapter V | wall plank hold → wall walk → chest-to-wall handstand hold → kick-up to wall → freestanding attempts |
| Planche (stretch goal) | Optional stretch item at the end of Forge B, from Chapter V; 2 short sets, about 5 min, skippable, never shown as missed | planche lean → frog stand → pseudo-planche push-up → tuck planche hold → advanced tuck hold → tuck planche push-up → straddle planche → planche push-up |

- **Stepping up:** criteria-gated, with an earliest chapter per step. The app shows "next step available" as information; the person chooses when to take it. A step not yet reached shows no "behind" message. Exact criteria per step are drafted in ticket 01 for the person's approval.
- **Passed** means a step's criteria were met.
- All track steps are available in the custom Forge.
- Realistic expectation: 36 weeks likely reaches tuck planche; the rest stays open past the campaign.

## Readiness gates

- **Shoulder pain ≥ 3:** overhead carry → front-hold carry; overhead press → band pull-apart. Offered as a calm choice, never automatic. Muscle-up steps follow this swap too.
- **Neck or shoulder pain ≥ 2:** handstand holds, wall walks, handstand push-up work and planche work swap to a pike hold or a regular push-up, as a choice.
- **Back pain ≥ 2 (Chapter IV on):** press stance rule above.
- Handstand and planche days add a short wrist warm-up.
- Existing rules hold: pain notes hold progression; no load increase after a reduced or pain session.

## The optional Forge

- Shown as a quiet card on the day's orders: "Optional Forge available."
- Eligible days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday. At most once a week. Never on a trial attempt day. **Not offered in a three-Forge make-up week.** Deload weeks and trial weeks are allowed; deload weeks show a "keep it light" note.
- It sits alongside the day's walk or restoration and replaces nothing.
- No hard block next to a Forge day; a quiet note appears if the chosen exercises repeat that day's or yesterday's main lifts.
- Once done, the card is gone for the week. If it is not done, nothing is said; it is never logged as missed or counted.
- **Chapter I:** the card opens Forge C.
- **Chapter II on:** the card opens the custom Forge builder:
  1. The full exercise library, grouped by pattern (lower body, push, pull, carry, core, arms, mobility). Tapping adds an exercise in tap order; tapping again removes it.
  2. "Start from last time's list" is offered as a shortcut, still editable.
  3. Done picking → the order editor (the existing one) to reorder.
  4. Begin. The standard warm-up is in front automatically and can be skipped.
  - Each exercise's default prescription is its prescription in the current chapter's Forge A or B, or a catalog default otherwise. The set count is editable in the builder.
  - Custom sessions are kept in history and shown on the exercise, but **do not** feed progression hints.

## Trials

- A track step **first passed during a chapter** is added to that chapter's trial as a part.
- Demonstrating that step **or the step just below it** counts.
- On a pain-gated day, the skill part can be marked "set aside for pain" without failing the trial.
- Trial content is otherwise unchanged.

- Skill tracks, gates and press stance as above.
