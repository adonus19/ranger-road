# Chapter I Forge and Gate Trial circuit proposal

**Status:** Superseded historical draft, 26 September 2026. Do not implement the exercises, doses, progression, or circuit below. The later [Chapter I content pack](../docs/rangers-road-full-program-content/01_THE_MUSTER.md) is authoritative and supplies different prescriptions.

## What the program set when this draft was written

Chapter I has strength on Monday and Thursday and names Forge A and Forge B, but does not assign one to each day. Its Gate Trial has a 2-mile brisk walk, a 3-round controlled circuit, a mind reflection, Psalm 121 with prayer, and a personal Ranger's Oath. Day 28 is a planning target, not a required trial date. A full Gate Trial waits for a Green readiness day. Yellow means roughly 25% less training volume and no load increase; Red means no strength mission. These rules come from [Chapters and Trials](../docs/CHAPTERS_AND_TRIALS.md) and the [Ranger's Road Program](../docs/RANGERS_ROAD_PROGRAM.md).

At the time of this draft, the docs did not name the Forge A/B exercises or the circuit stations and reps. Every movement proposed below appeared by name in the [Exercise Library Specification](../docs/EXERCISE_LIBRARY_SPEC.md). The assignments, set counts, rep counts, equipment assumptions, and completion standard below were choices for review and have since been superseded.

## Proposed Forge prescription

Use Forge A on Monday and Forge B on Thursday throughout Chapter I. A missed day does not add work to the next session. The aim is controlled, repeatable movement: choose a resistance, box height, and push-up hand height that allow clean reps without rising pain, with the final set of each movement at or below RPE 7. Rest as needed to keep that standard. Record the actual load or setup rather than suggesting an automatic increase.

The first exposure to each workout is **one set per movement**. On a later Green exposure, offer a second set only if the previous exposure to that same workout completed every prescribed movement and set, had no pain event or reduction, left symptoms stable, and finished each movement at or below RPE 7. The person chooses whether to take that second set. Two sets is the Chapter I ceiling in this proposal.

On Yellow, keep two sets only if the immediately preceding exposure to that workout was a clean, unreduced two-set Green session; otherwise use one set. Apply 75% to the chosen rep target for every set and round to the nearest whole rep: 5 becomes 4, 6 becomes 5, and 8 becomes 6. Do not increase load. A Red day has no Forge workout. Any rising pain uses the documented Pain flow and is recorded.

Reps in the tables are **per set**. "Each side" means the listed reps on each side, with one set counting both sides.

### Forge A — Monday

| Movement | Reps per set | Proposed setup |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Box squat | 8 | Bodyweight to a stable box or chair at a comfortable depth |
| Push-up | 5–8 | Hands on a stable elevated surface if needed for clean, comfortable reps |
| One-arm DB row | 8 each side | Light dumbbell; free hand supported on a stable surface |
| Bird dog | 5 each side | Unweighted, controlled movement |

### Forge B — Thursday

| Movement | Reps per set | Proposed setup |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Supported split squat | 6 each side | Hold a stable support; choose a comfortable depth |
| Push-up | 5–8 | Use the same documented setup options as Forge A |
| One-arm DB row | 8 each side | Light dumbbell; free hand supported |
| Glute bridge | 8 | Unweighted, controlled movement |

This repeats the push and pull patterns on both strength days so the movements can be learned and compared without needing a pull-up station. It assumes access to a sturdy box or chair, a secure surface for the push-up and row, a light dumbbell, and floor space. Equipment has not been confirmed. If there is no dumbbell, the pulling movement needs a documented alternative before this can become the program; a substitute should not appear without its own guidance and media.

The elevated push-up is a proposed regression of the named push-up, not a separate scored exercise. Its exact hand supports and form cues need to be specified in the exercise guide before release. Assisted pull-up is a possible Forge B alternative if a suitable bar and assistance are available. I have left it out of the main prescription until the equipment and comfortable starting assistance are known. The current program permits reducing band assistance only after three clean sets at the top of the range, which this introductory one- or two-set plan would not reach.

## Proposed Gate Trial circuit

Complete **three rounds** of the following stations on a Green day. Use a controlled pace and rest as needed between stations or rounds. All five trial phases belong to one dated attempt, with the walk and circuit on that Green date. The phases can be completed in any order; this proposal sets the order of movements *within each circuit round* only. There is no time target or speed score for the circuit.

| Station | Work in each round | What to record |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Box squat | 8 reps | Box height or setup; completed reps |
| Push-up | 5 clean reps | Hand height or support; completed reps |
| One-arm DB row | 8 reps each side | Dumbbell load; completed reps on each side |
| Bird dog | 5 reps each side | Completed reps on each side |

These are Forge A movements, so someone who has trained Forge A will have practiced every station. The documented Day 1 trial option remains open; the trial's Quick Help must also work for a first attempt. A chosen box height, push-up support, or dumbbell load is recorded for comparison, but the proposal does not set a minimum load or floor push-up requirement. A lighter row load or higher push-up support chosen without a pain trigger can still count if all reps are completed with controlled form and the change is recorded. If pain rises, a concerning symptom appears, a station is skipped, or reps remain short, stop and save the work as an attempt; leave the Gate Trial incomplete.

The trial still requires the full 2-mile brisk walk, all three circuit rounds, mind reflection, Psalm 121 with prayer, and the personal oath. It may be attempted on or after Day 1; this circuit proposal does not add a Day 28 gate. The post-mission function answer belongs 60–120 minutes after the effort, as the existing [UX specification](../docs/UX_SPEC.md) says.

## Why this is a modest starting point

The [2026 ACSM position stand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/) supports training major muscle groups at least twice weekly and adapting resistance work to the person. It reviews healthy adults; it does not prescribe these movements or doses for someone with recurring pain. [HHS physical activity guidance](https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/about-physical-activity-guidelines/questions-answers) advises inactive adults to start small and build gradually. The one-set entry, second-set ceiling, station choices, and trial reps are product proposals based on the Ranger's Road constraints, not numbers taken from those sources.

## Decisions needed before implementation

1. Confirm the proposed Monday = Forge A and Thursday = Forge B pairing, movement lists, and one-set entry with an optional second set. Choose one default Forge B pull: repeat DB row or assisted pull-up. If assisted pull-up is chosen, its band assistance would stay unchanged under this one- or two-set Chapter I plan unless the authoritative progression rule changes.
2. Confirm the equipment that can be counted on: a stable box or chair, push-up support, light dumbbell, and any pull-up bar or bands.
3. Confirm the four trial stations and reps, the one-date Green trial attempt, and whether an elevated push-up or lighter row still counts when all reps and rounds are completed cleanly.
4. Confirm that an incomplete circuit is saved as an attempt, with its pain and station results, while the Gate Trial remains incomplete.

After review, the approved prescription belongs in the authoritative program and trial docs before it is seeded. The workout and trial definitions should carry a content version and completed sessions should keep their definition snapshots, so later changes cannot rewrite history. The active Forge and trial screens need Quick Help and generated start/mid/end images for every named movement, with pain guidance available in the workout, including a Day 1 trial attempt.

The existing trial result records only round count. New records would need per-round station reps and setup/load, pain events and actions, and a status that separates attempts from completed results. Each attempt should keep its readiness link and definition snapshot. The Gate Trial is a major mission, so the new flow also needs terrain, RPE, and recovery fields under the [program's tracking rules](../docs/RANGERS_ROAD_PROGRAM.md). Older saved results must remain readable.
