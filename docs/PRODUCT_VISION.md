# Product Vision

## Working Name

**The Ranger's Road**

## Mission

Build a private, low-friction training companion that guides one person through a 9-month transformation in body, mind, faith, leadership, and practical capability.

The user should never need to ask:
- What am I supposed to do today?
- How do I perform this exercise?
- Why am I doing this?
- What happens if I am tired or hurting?
- What am I training toward?

The app answers those questions immediately.

## User

Primary user:
- 44-year-old husband and father
- 5'10", ~225 lb at baseline
- target ~195 lb
- wants improved energy, endurance, waist reduction, functional strength, mobility, and usefulness
- previously athletic, currently deconditioned
- recurring lower-back pain
- occasional shoulder/neck pain
- dislikes indefinite programs
- highly motivated by measurable finish lines and competition
- Christian
- wants to lead family well
- wants practical/outdoor capability
- prefers themed progression
- needs low-friction UX

The architecture should allow future users, but the first product should remain optimized for one primary user.

## North Star

The user becomes more capable in real life:
- walk and hike farther
- recover faster
- carry a child
- work outside for hours
- remain functional after exertion
- handle household demands with more energy
- lead family with greater consistency and presence
- build spiritual and reflective habits
- learn practical skills

## Five Pillars

1. **Body**
2. **Mind**
3. **Faith**
4. **Leadership**
5. **Fieldcraft**

## Physical Attributes

- Might
- Endurance
- Movement
- Resolve

These can use measurable progress.

## Non-Numeric Formation Domains

- Faith
- Hearth / family leadership
- Wisdom

These should use practices, milestones, reflections, missions, and chapter completion rather than fake scores.

## Product Tone

- grounded
- capable
- restrained
- masculine without caricature
- adventurous without cosplay
- Christian without becoming a devotional streak app
- challenging without shame
- visually inspired by classic ranger/trail/field-journal aesthetics

## Experience Goal

Opening the app should feel like opening a personal field journal and mission board, not a spreadsheet.

Primary home action:
**BEGIN TODAY'S MISSION**
