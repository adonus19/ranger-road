# Faith and Leadership

## Principle

Faith is not numerically scored.

Leadership is not domination.

The program should train:
- self-government
- service
- integrity
- presence
- listening
- responsibility
- courage
- emotional regulation
- stewardship
- patience
- consistency
- wisdom
- teaching
- stability

## Daily Faith Rhythm

### Morning Watch
5–10 min:
- Scripture
- prayer
- review orders
- prompt: "What does my family need from me today?"

### Evening Watch
3–5 min:
- win
- failure / missed standard
- gratitude
- tomorrow

## Weekly Formation Pattern

Scripture -> reflection -> prayer -> action

## Family Leadership Principles

- lead yourself first
- listen before solving
- serve without requiring praise
- own responsibilities fully
- admit mistakes
- repair quickly
- keep promises
- reduce household friction
- teach without humiliating
- remain stable under fatigue
- protect family time
- model what you ask of others

## Hearth Missions

Examples:
- ask spouse what would genuinely help this week
- own a recurring household task
- plan a family outing
- spend an hour with phone away
- give daughter undivided attention
- teach a practical skill
- remain present for 30 minutes when tired
- handle logistics so others can simply enjoy the activity
