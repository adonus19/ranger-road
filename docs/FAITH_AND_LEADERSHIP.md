# Faith and Leadership

## Principle

Faith is not numerically scored.

Leadership is not domination.

The program should train:
- self-government
- service
- integrity
- presence
- listening
- responsibility
- courage
- emotional regulation
- stewardship
- patience
- consistency
- wisdom
- teaching
- stability

## Daily Faith Rhythm

### Morning Watch
5–10 min:
- Scripture
- prayer
- on Monday (or a midweek Day 1), the week's leadership lesson, about 3 minutes
- review orders
- prompt: "What does my family need from me today?"

### Evening Watch
3–5 min:
- win
- failure / missed standard
- gratitude
- tomorrow

## Weekly Formation Pattern

Scripture -> reflection -> prayer -> action

## Weekly Leadership Lesson

Leadership is part of the normal training week, not a separate course:

- Each week has one short lesson built on one or two of the principles below and the week's Scripture. It is read in Monday's Morning Watch.
- The week's Hearth mission is the lesson's practice. Keep shows the mission all week and opens the lesson.
- Where the week has a Sunday reflection question, it looks back on the lesson.
- Each lesson ties into the current book and suggests one book for later. The reading plan keeps one book at a time, with no deadline.
- Nothing about the lessons is scored.

The approved lessons, the principles with their Scripture and practice, and the reading plan are in [Field Manual Content](rangers-road-full-program-content/FIELD_MANUAL.md). Chapter III's lessons (Weeks 9–12, Calm Decisions) were approved 2026-11-09 and are built; later chapters get theirs when their content is built.

## Family Leadership Principles

- lead yourself first
- listen before solving
- serve without requiring praise
- own responsibilities fully
- admit mistakes
- repair quickly
- keep promises
- reduce household friction
- teach without humiliating
- remain stable under fatigue
- protect family time
- model what you ask of others

## Hearth Missions

Examples:
- ask spouse what would genuinely help this week
- own a recurring household task
- plan a family outing
- spend an hour with phone away
- give daughter undivided attention
- teach a practical skill
- remain present for 30 minutes when tired
- handle logistics so others can simply enjoy the activity

Chapter III's Hearth missions: Week 9 (four parts: ask what feels stuck and carry a piece, finish one tedious recurring task daily, tell your daughter about nearly quitting, the family prays for one awaited thing at a meal each day), Week 10 (one avoided conversation), Week 11 (plan a family outing), Week 12 (tell your spouse the plan and turnaround, invite the family with a job for your daughter, family prayer for the climb and weather, tell your spouse your high-point answer). The chapter's leadership lessons use the Calm Decisions framework: Stop, Observe, Prioritize, Decide, Communicate, Reassess.
