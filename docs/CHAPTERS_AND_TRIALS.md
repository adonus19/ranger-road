# Chapters and Trials

## Trial Timing

A chapter's trial comes after its last full week, not inside it, so the person starts the trial rested rather than at the tired end of a week.

**Any-day trials (decided 2026-11-09, for every trial; the Gate Trial joined the same day, before it was passed).** Once a chapter's last week has ended, the person may start its trial on any day. The content pack's day is shown only as a suggestion.

- The trial replaces that day's main order (Forge, walk or Restoration). Nothing is added on top.
- There is no required gap between attempts. The person tries again when they feel able.
- A trial is passed when every part is finished as written on a Green day: the full distances and rounds, the written reflections, the prayer, and the oath, without pain at 3 or more and without a changed movement. There is no time standard; the program does not race.
- The trial's own rules apply on whichever day is picked, including pieces that need separate days (the High Country Trial's hike and circuit) and the Green check on each physical piece.
- A Yellow or Red morning, a missed day, or a stopped attempt is simply not a pass that day. Stopped attempts and their pain notes stay in history. A trial postponed for weather is recorded as a postponement, never a failed attempt.
- Days between attempts repeat the chapter's final week, Forges included at that week's dose. Nothing new or harder is added while a trial is pending, and no load rises.
- The next chapter begins only after a pass. After a Monday pass it continues that same week from Tuesday, because the trial took the place of its first strength day. After a pass on any other day it begins the following Monday, and the days in between repeat the previous chapter's final week.
- A make-up never delays a trial. When a trial is started on a day that holds a moved Forge session, the trial takes the day and the moved session is dropped (confirmed 2026-11-09).
- Each week of retries moves every later chapter back by a week. Monthly check-ins stay anchored to Day 1.
- The Gate Trial first used Monday and Thursday attempt days. It moved to this rule on 2026-11-09, before it was passed, because those days did not fit the person's schedule. Drafts, attempts and results saved under the earlier rule read unchanged.

Content packs may still list a trial on a named day. That is a suggestion only. Each trial's page states its parts; the High Country Trial and its stand-in are below.

## Skill Parts in Trials

Skill tracks are described in `RANGERS_ROAD_PROGRAM.md` (Skill Tracks). A track step adds a part to a trial only once the person has passed it.

- A track step first passed during a chapter is added to that chapter's trial as a part. A step passed in an earlier chapter is not added again.
- The part is passed by demonstrating that step or the step just below it. Doing the step below is not a changed movement for this rule.
- On a pain-gated day (see Readiness Gates in `RANGERS_ROAD_PROGRAM.md`), the person may mark the skill part "Set aside for pain" without failing the trial. Neck or shoulder pain at 2 still allows a Green day. Pain at 3 or more makes the day Yellow, so the whole trial waits for a Green day as usual.
- The rest of each trial is unchanged.
- Before a track is live: A track adds trial parts only from the chapter its Forge slot starts in (the pull and push tracks from Chapter II). A step passed before then, such as Chapter I's assisted pull-up or push-up, joins that first chapter's trial once. So the Gate Trial has no skill parts, and a step-1 pass from Chapter I is a part of the Three-Mile Trial. (Settled in ticket 09, waiting on the person's confirmation.)
- Pain-gated days: A step's gate is the Readiness Gate that swaps it: neck or shoulder pain at 2 or more for the feet-elevated pike push-up, both wall handstand push-up steps, every handstand step and every planche step; shoulder pain at 3 or more for the muscle-up steps. The assisted, band-light, bodyweight and chest-to-bar pull-ups, the push-up and the pike push-up have no gate, so their part cannot be set aside.
- The parts are fixed when an attempt starts. A step first passed after that joins the next attempt. Going back a step on the track does not remove a part.

The [Chapter I content pack](rangers-road-full-program-content/01_THE_MUSTER.md) supplies the exact Forge A/B prescriptions, daily walking doses, six circuit stations, and trial prompts. Complete the Gate Trial phases in the documented order: Road, Gate Circuit, Mind, Spirit, then Oath. Rest 5–10 minutes after the walk before starting the circuit; at least 5 minutes is required, and longer than 10 minutes is allowed when needed to recover safely. Spend 10–15 quiet minutes in prayer; at least 10 minutes is required, and longer is allowed. Record the actual rest and prayer durations on a new completed trial result. The circuit is controlled; its completion time is recorded without a speed target.

The Gate Trial follows the Trial Timing rule above, including the any-day rule (from 2026-11-09). It opens the Monday after Week 4, the suggested day: campaign day 29 for a Monday Day 1, or day 30–35 when a short lead-in follows a Tuesday–Sunday start. From then it can be taken on any day until it is passed, and the dashboard countdown runs to that first day. Until the pass, every day repeats Week 4, its lighter Forges included (no load increases). 2 Timothy 4:7 is read on the day it is taken. Week 4 Saturday is a 30-minute easy walk plus preparation for the trial: choose the 2-mile route and set out the box, pull-up band, and carry weight. Chapter I is complete once the Gate Trial is passed; Chapter II then begins as the Trial Timing rule describes.

The Three-Mile Trial follows the Trial Timing rule above, including the any-day rule: once Week 8 is over it can be started on any day, with the Monday after Week 8 as the suggestion. Week 8 Saturday is a 30-minute easy walk plus preparation for Monday: choose the 3-mile route, set out the 30-lb suitcase carry weight, and find three flights of stairs. Days between attempts repeat Week 8, Forges included at Week 8's dose. Chapter III begins only after a pass: after a Monday pass from Tuesday, otherwise the following Monday.

Week 8 Monday (Forge A) keeps the written sets and reps with no load increases. Week 8 Thursday (Forge B) is about 25% lighter, around 21 of its 28 work sets (curl and chin-up included) chosen by the person; like Week 4, this is a guide and not a hard limit on saving.

Pack: [The High Country](rangers-road-full-program-content/03_THE_HIGH_COUNTRY.md). It follows the any-day rule above, with these settled details (2026-11-09):

- **Pieces and days.** The hike and the circuit are on separate days. Each needs a Green day with no pain at 3 or more on the morning check. On the trail the turnaround is at pain 4 or more (decided 2026-11-09): pain at 3 during the hike is recorded but does not stop it, while pain at 4 or more, or a changed movement, ends that attempt. The circuit keeps the usual limit of 3. A passed hike stays passed; the circuit may be done on any later Green day. Forty-eight hours is a target note, not enforced. The hike takes the day's order; the circuit takes a Forge if it falls on one. Week 12 Saturday is preparation: an easy walk, choose the trail, check the forecast, pack.
- **Weather.** The hike wants a clear-weather day ("pick a clear-weather day"). The person can mark an attempt "postponed for weather". Turning back under the Navigation II turnaround criteria is also a postponement. Neither is a failed attempt. A stop for pain is a stopped attempt as usual.
- **Winter stand-in.** Allowed any time, as the person's choice, with an optional "weather reason" field. It is one continuous walk of 1.5–2 hours. No treadmill: elevation gain comes from outdoor terrain (local gentle hills, stairs) when possible, otherwise an indoor track, and gain is recorded, not required. Navigation is on paper with a real trail map (route, junctions, distance, gain, turnaround), with location estimates at set checkpoints. The 5 quiet minutes and the reflection come at the halfway mark.
- **Skill parts** follow Skill Parts in Trials below, after the circuit.
- **No photo.** The trial has no photo step. Photos are not part of the app's trials.
- **Hearth part (d).** Week 12's "after the trial, tell your spouse your answer to the high-point question" shows on Keep from the day the hike is finished until it is marked Done, or until Chapter IV begins (decided 2026-11-09).
