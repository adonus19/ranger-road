# Nutrition and Recovery

## Goal

Support fat loss, recovery, energy, and long-term adherence without requiring obsessive tracking.

## Initial Targets

- protein at 3 meals/day
- fruit/vegetable at least 3 times/day
- reduce sweetened tea gradually
- water before lunch and dinner
- 3 alcohol-free evenings/week
- stop eating at comfortable satisfaction
- 7+ hours sleep when practical

## Escalation

Only introduce calorie tracking if:
- weight trend stalls for multiple weeks
- waist is not changing
- adherence to simple rules is established

## Alcohol

Treat alcohol as intentional rather than automatic.

Initial Month 1 behavior experiment:
- minimum 3 alcohol-free evenings/week
- before couch/rest: change clothes, drink water, 5–10 minute walk

## Recovery Inputs

Track:
- sleep duration
- subjective energy
- pain
- training RPE
- post-mission function

## Post-Mission Function

After major efforts, assess 60–120 minutes later:
- energy
- soreness
- irritability
- ability to help at home
- ability to participate in normal family life

This is a first-class metric.

## Safety

The app should never imply that pain must be pushed through.

Persistent or worsening symptoms should surface a recommendation to seek appropriate medical evaluation.
