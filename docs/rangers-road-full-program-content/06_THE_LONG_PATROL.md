# Endurance Nutrition / Hydration
Practice:
- begin hydrated
- bring water appropriate to conditions
- small familiar carbohydrate snack on longer outings
- avoid experimenting with new foods during trials

# Fieldcraft — First Aid I
Study/practice:
- scene safety
- call 911 / summon help
- bleeding control basics
- simple wound care
- sprain basics
- heat exhaustion / dehydration warning signs
- recognize situations requiring professional care

The app must state this does not replace hands-on CPR/first-aid certification.

# Daughter Quest
2–3 mile adventure if appropriate and enjoyable. Let daughter help choose route, check weather, carry water, and pack snacks.

# Long Patrol Trial
1. Complete 5-mile walk/hike at sustainable pace.
2. Record distance, time, elevation, HR if available, RPE, symptoms, recovery.
3. Perform at least two navigation checks during route.
4. Complete one defined light carry block.
5. Spend 10 quiet minutes during route.
6. At 60–120 min after completion record Post-Mission Function:
   - energy
   - soreness
   - irritability
   - ability to help at home
   - ability to participate with family
