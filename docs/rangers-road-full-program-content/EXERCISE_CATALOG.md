# Exercise Catalog

Every named movement in the app should have a Quick Help view and a Full Guide. The generated visual system should use 3–5 consistent illustrated frames plus a front/back muscle map.

## Box Squat

**Purpose:** Build lower-body strength and controlled squat mechanics while allowing depth to be limited.

**Movement pattern:** Squat.

**Targets:** Quads and glutes primarily; adductors and trunk stabilizers secondarily.

**How:** Stand in front of a stable box/bench, feet about shoulder width. Brace. Sit back and down under control until you lightly contact the box. Do not collapse or fully relax. Drive through the whole foot to stand.

**Feel:** Quads, glutes, and trunk bracing.

**Avoid:** Crashing onto box, knees collapsing inward, weight rolling onto toes, losing trunk tension.

**Pain-aware options:** Raise box height, reduce load, use bodyweight.

---

## Bench Press

**Purpose:** Build horizontal pressing strength.

**Movement pattern:** Horizontal push.

**Targets:** Chest and triceps primarily; front delts and upper-back stabilizers secondarily.

**How:** Eyes roughly under bar. Plant feet. Pull shoulder blades gently back/down. Lower bar under control toward lower chest/sternum. Press up without bouncing.

**Feel:** Chest, triceps, stable upper back.

**Avoid:** Excessive elbow flare, bar drifting toward neck, bouncing, loose feet/shoulders.

**Pain-aware options:** Reduce load/range, slightly narrow grip, substitute push-up or dumbbell floor press.

---

## Assisted Pull-Up

**Purpose:** Develop vertical pulling strength toward unassisted pull-ups.

**Targets:** Lats and upper back; biceps, grip, and core.

**How:** Secure assistance band. Begin in an active hang. Pull elbows down and back. Bring chin toward/over bar if comfortable. Lower slowly.

**Feel:** Lats, upper back, biceps, grip.

**Avoid:** Swinging, shrugging, crashing down.

**Progression:** Reduce band assistance after clean sets at top of rep range.

---

## Supported Split Squat

**Purpose:** Build unilateral leg strength and balance.

**Targets:** Front-leg quads and glutes; hamstrings/adductors/core secondarily.

**How:** Split stance while lightly holding rack for balance. Lower vertically to comfortable depth. Push through front foot to rise.

**Feel:** Front quad and glute.

**Avoid:** Front knee collapsing inward, excessive forward lean, bouncing.

**Pain-aware options:** Shorten range, use more support, static split-stance hold.

---

## Split Squat

**Purpose:** Build single-leg strength and balance without holding a rack. Chapter II's step-up from Supported Split Squat.

**Movement pattern:** Lunge / split stance.

**Targets:** Front-leg quads and glutes; hamstrings, adductors, and core secondarily.

**How:** Take a long split stance with feet hip-width apart, front foot flat and back heel lifted. Keep your torso tall and brace. Lower straight down under control until the back knee is just above the floor or as deep as feels comfortable. Push through the whole front foot to rise. Finish all reps on one side, then switch.

**Feel:** Front quad and glute, with some stretch in the back hip.

**Avoid:** Front knee collapsing inward, leaning far forward, bouncing out of the bottom, a stance so short the front heel lifts.

**Pain-aware options:** Shorten the range, touch a hand to a wall or rack for balance, or go back to Supported Split Squat.

**Step-up from:** Supported Split Squat.

---

## Suitcase Carry

**Purpose:** Build grip and anti-side-bend trunk strength.

**Targets:** Obliques, grip, upper back, glutes.

**How:** Hold one weight at side. Stand tall and walk slowly without leaning or twisting.

**Feel:** Side of trunk, grip, upper back, hips.

**Avoid:** Leaning away from load, shrugging, rushing.

---

## Side Plank

**Purpose:** Build lateral trunk stability.

**Targets:** Obliques, glute medius, shoulder stabilizers.

**How:** Elbow under shoulder. Lift hips into straight line and breathe normally.

**Feel:** Side of trunk and hip.

**Pain-aware option:** Bend knees; shorten hold.

---

## Goblet Squat to Box

**Purpose:** Reinforce squat pattern with front-loaded counterbalance.

**Targets:** Quads, glutes, core.

**How:** Hold dumbbell at chest. Brace, sit down under control to box, lightly touch, stand.

**Feel:** Quads, glutes, upper trunk tension.

---

## Goblet Squat

**Purpose:** Build squat strength with a front-loaded counterbalance and no box to rest on. Chapter II's step-up from Goblet Squat to Box.

**Movement pattern:** Squat.

**Targets:** Quads, glutes, core.

**How:** Hold one dumbbell against your chest with both hands. Stand with feet about shoulder width. Brace, then sit down and back under control to a comfortable depth, keeping heels flat and chest tall. Drive through the whole foot to stand.

**Feel:** Quads, glutes, upper trunk tension.

**Avoid:** Heels lifting, knees collapsing inward, rounding forward, dropping quickly into the bottom.

**Pain-aware options:** Squat to a box or bench again, shorten the depth, or lighten the dumbbell.

**Step-up from:** Goblet Squat to Box.

---

## Step-Up

**Purpose:** Build climbing and hiking strength.

**Targets:** Quads and glutes primarily; calves/core secondarily.

**How:** Entire foot on stable step. Drive through top leg to stand. Control descent.

**Feel:** Leg on the step.

**Avoid:** Jumping off trailing leg, dropping quickly, step too high.

---

## One-Arm Dumbbell Row

**Purpose:** Build upper-back pulling strength and posture.

**Targets:** Lats/mid-back; biceps, rear delts, grip.

**How:** Support one hand on bench. Maintain neutral spine. Pull dumbbell toward hip/ribcage and lower slowly.

**Feel:** Lat and mid-back.

**Avoid:** Torso rotation and jerking.

---

## Push-Up

**Purpose:** Build practical pressing and trunk strength.

**Targets:** Chest/triceps; shoulders/core.

**How:** Rigid plank. Hands just outside shoulders. Lower under control with elbows angled back. Press while maintaining body line.

**Feel:** Chest, triceps, core.

**Avoid:** Sagging hips, craning neck, excessive elbow flare.

---

## Glute Bridge

**Purpose:** Build hip extension and glute activation.

**Targets:** Glutes primarily; hamstrings/core secondarily.

**How:** Lie on back, knees bent. Brace lightly. Drive through feet to raise hips without over-arching back.

**Feel:** Glutes, some hamstrings.

**Avoid:** Feeling the movement mainly in lower back.

---

## Farmer Carry

**Purpose:** Build full-body carrying capacity, grip, and posture.

**Targets:** Grip, traps, trunk; glutes/legs.

**How:** Hold weights at both sides. Stand tall, ribs controlled, walk steadily.

**Feel:** Grip, upper back, trunk, whole-body tension.

---

## Bird Dog

**Purpose:** Build trunk stability and cross-body coordination.

**Targets:** Deep trunk stabilizers, glutes, shoulder stabilizers.

**How:** Hands/knees. Extend opposite arm and leg without twisting or arching. Pause, return, alternate.

**Feel:** Mild core and glute engagement rather than a large burn.

---

## Standing Calf Raise

**Purpose:** Build calf and ankle capacity for walking/hiking.

**How:** Rise onto balls of feet under control, pause, lower slowly.

**Feel:** Calves.

**Avoid:** Bouncing.

---

## Single-Leg Balance

**Purpose:** Improve ankle/hip stability and uneven-ground confidence.

**How:** Stand on one foot with soft knee, tall posture, steady breathing.

**Progression:** Longer holds, reaches, light load, uneven but safe surface.

**Harder versions (Chapter V on, in Forge B's balance slot):** eyes closed, slow head turns side to side, or standing on a soft pad (a folded mat or cushion). Take one at a time, and keep a wall or rack within reach.

**Equipment:** None; a folded mat for the soft-pad version.

**Images:** Text only.

---

## Slow Step-Down

**Purpose:** Prepare knees/quads for downhill hiking.

**Targets:** Quads, glute medius, knee/hip control.

**How:** Stand on low step. Lower opposite heel toward floor over ~3 seconds. Keep knee tracking with toes. Return.

**Feel:** Working leg quad and outer hip.

---

## Supported Reverse Lunge

**Purpose:** Build single-leg strength with controlled stepping.

**How:** Step one foot backward, lower vertically, drive through front foot to return. Use rack for balance if needed.

**Feel:** Front glute/quad.

---

## Front-Hold Carry

**Purpose:** Build trunk/arm endurance relevant to holding a child or object in front.

**How:** Hold one dumbbell or safe load close to chest. Walk tall.

**Feel:** Arms, upper back, trunk.

---

## Bear-Hug Carry

**Purpose:** Build awkward-load carrying capacity.

**How:** Hug a securely packed object close to torso. Walk with short controlled steps.

**Feel:** Arms, upper back, trunk, legs.

**Safety:** Object must be secure and non-shifting.

---

## Elevated Light Hinge Pattern

**Purpose:** Teach safe hip-hinge mechanics without heavy deadlifting.

**How:** Start an empty/light bar from elevated safeties/blocks. Soften knees, push hips backward while keeping bar close and spine neutral. Stand by driving hips forward.

**Feel:** Glutes and hamstrings with trunk bracing.

**Stop/substitute:** If lower-back symptoms increase.

---

## Hammer Curl

**Purpose:** Optional light arm work that supports carrying and pulling. It is an add-on to Forge B and never replaces a main exercise.

**Movement pattern:** Elbow flexion.

**Targets:** Biceps and the forearm muscles that help grip.

**How:** Stand tall holding a light dumbbell in each hand with palms facing your thighs. Keep elbows beside your ribs. Bend the elbows to raise the weights until your thumbs approach your shoulders, then lower slowly. Keep the wrists straight.

**Feel:** Front of the upper arm and forearm.

**Avoid:** Swinging the body, letting elbows drift forward, leaning back, going heavy enough that form breaks.

**Pain-aware options:** Lighter weights, one arm at a time, sit on a bench, or skip it; it is optional.

**Forge expansion:** The hammer curl becomes required and light: in Forge A every chapter from Chapter II, and in the curl rotation of Chapter I's Forge C. Its Purpose and Pain-aware lines change when the Chapter II rebalance (ticket 05) ships.

**Equipment:** Dumbbells.

**Images:** Existing.

---

## Cat-Camel

**Purpose:** Gentle spinal movement and awareness.

**How:** On hands/knees, slowly alternate comfortable spinal flexion and extension.

**Rule:** Gentle range only; not a forceful stretch.

---

## Half-Kneeling Hip-Flexor Stretch

**Purpose:** Improve hip-extension mobility.

**How:** Half kneel. Lightly tuck pelvis and shift forward until front of rear hip stretches.

**Feel:** Front of rear hip, not lower back.

---

## Supported Squat Hold

**Purpose:** Gradually improve squat-position comfort/mobility.

**How:** Hold rack or stable support and descend only to comfortable depth. Breathe while maintaining whole-foot contact.

---

## Supported Deep Squat

**Purpose:** Improve comfort in a deep squat position by holding it for time. Chapter II's step-up from Supported Squat Hold.

**Targets:** Hips, ankles, and the whole lower body, with trunk position.

**How:** Hold a rack or stable support in front of you. Lower into the deepest squat that feels comfortable, with your whole foot on the floor and your chest up. Breathe slowly and stay relaxed for the hold, then stand up.

**Feel:** Stretch through the hips, groin, and ankles; no sharp or pinching pain at the knee or hip.

**Avoid:** Heels lifting, bouncing, forcing depth, holding your breath, pain at the knee or hip.

**Pain-aware options:** Use more support, sit higher, shorten the hold, or go back to Supported Squat Hold.

**Step-up from:** Supported Squat Hold.

---

## Wall Slide

**Purpose:** Encourage shoulder-blade control and overhead mobility.

**How:** Back against wall as tolerated. Slide arms upward without forcing range.

**Feel:** Upper back/shoulder movement, not sharp shoulder pain.

---

## Open-Book Rotation

**Purpose:** Thoracic rotation mobility.

**How:** Lie on side with knees bent. Rotate top arm/chest open while keeping knees together.

**Feel:** Mid/upper-back rotation.

---

## Ankle Rock

**Purpose:** Improve ankle dorsiflexion for squats and walking.

**How:** Keep heel down and gently drive knee forward over toes.

**Feel:** Ankle/calf, not knee pain.

---

## Neutral-Spine Hamstring Stretch

**Purpose:** Improve posterior-chain flexibility without rounding low back aggressively.

**How:** Hinge from hips toward a straightened leg while keeping spine neutral.

**Feel:** Back of thigh.

---

# Overhead Press

## Seated Two-DB Overhead Press

**Purpose:** Build overhead pressing strength with the back supported. The first overhead press in the program.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical push.

**Targets:** Shoulders (front and side delts) and triceps; upper back and trunk secondarily.

**How:** Set the bench upright and sit with your back against it, feet flat. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms forward or slightly turned in. Brace lightly. Press both weights up until the arms are straight and the weights sit over the shoulders, then lower under control to shoulder height.

**Feel:** Shoulders and back of the upper arms.

**Avoid:** Arching the low back off the bench, pressing the weights forward instead of up, shrugging hard, banging the dumbbells together.

**Pain-aware options:** Lighter weights, a shorter range that stops before any pinch, palms turned in. On shoulder pain at 3 or more, swap to Band Pull-Apart (see Readiness gates in `RANGERS_ROAD_PROGRAM.md`).

**Equipment:** Adjustable bench (upright), dumbbells.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 8.

---

## Standing Two-DB Overhead Press

**Purpose:** Overhead pressing that also trains the trunk to stay tall without a backrest.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical push.

**Targets:** Shoulders and triceps; trunk, glutes and upper back secondarily.

**How:** Stand with feet about hip width, a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height. Squeeze the glutes and keep the ribs down. Press both weights straight up until the arms are straight, then lower under control. Keep the legs still; this is not a push press.

**Feel:** Shoulders, triceps and a braced trunk.

**Avoid:** Leaning back, letting the ribs flare, dipping the knees to drive the weight.

**Pain-aware options:** Sit on the upright bench instead. With back pain at 2 or more from Chapter IV, the press is done seated or half-kneeling (see Press stance in `RANGERS_ROAD_PROGRAM.md`).

**Equipment:** Dumbbells.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 8.

---

## One-Arm DB Overhead Press

**Purpose:** Press one side at a time, which lets each shoulder work through its own comfortable path and asks the trunk to resist leaning.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical push, one side.

**Targets:** Shoulder and triceps of the working arm; obliques and upper back secondarily.

**How:** Seated on the upright bench or standing. Hold one dumbbell at shoulder height, palm forward or turned in. Brace, press straight up until the arm is straight, and lower under control. Finish all reps on one side, then switch.

**Feel:** The working shoulder and the side of the trunk opposite the weight.

**Avoid:** Leaning away from the weight, shrugging, rushing the lowering.

**Pain-aware options:** Sit, use a lighter weight, or shorten the range. On shoulder pain at 3 or more, swap to Band Pull-Apart.

**Equipment:** Dumbbell; adjustable bench for the seated version.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 8 per side.

---

## Half-Kneeling One-Arm DB Press

**Purpose:** A one-arm press that keeps the low back quiet. Offered from Chapter III, and one of the two stances on a back-pain day from Chapter IV.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical push, one side.

**Targets:** Shoulder and triceps; trunk and the glute of the down knee secondarily.

**How:** Kneel on one knee on a pad with the other foot flat in front. Hold the dumbbell on the side of the down knee, at shoulder height; the palm may face in. Squeeze the glute of the down knee and stand tall through the hips. Press straight up and lower under control. Switch the stance when you switch arms.

**Feel:** The working shoulder, the trunk, and the glute of the down knee.

**Avoid:** Arching the back, leaning to one side, a knee that hurts on a hard floor.

**Pain-aware options:** More padding under the knee, a lighter weight, or the seated one-arm press. Half-kneeling is one-arm only.

**Equipment:** Dumbbell, a pad or folded mat for the knee.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 8 per side.

---

## Barbell Overhead Press

**Purpose:** Build overhead strength with a load that can rise in small steps. Forge A's press from Chapter V on.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical push.

**Targets:** Shoulders and triceps; upper back, trunk and glutes secondarily.

**How:** Set the bar in the rack at about collarbone height. Grip just outside the shoulders, step under it, and unrack so it rests on the front of the shoulders. Take one or two steps back. Squeeze the glutes and brace. Press the bar straight up, moving your head back just enough for the bar to pass your face, then bring your head through once the bar passes your forehead. Lower to the front of the shoulders under control. Rack it when done.

**Feel:** Shoulders, triceps and a braced trunk.

**Avoid:** Leaning back to finish the rep, pressing the bar out in front, unracking from too low, dipping the knees.

**Pain-aware options:** Lighter load, a narrower range, or two dumbbells. With back pain at 2 or more from Chapter IV, use the seated barbell press with lighter load or swap to dumbbells (see Press stance). On shoulder pain at 3 or more, swap to Band Pull-Apart.

**Equipment:** Barbell and rack.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 5.

---

## Seated Barbell Overhead Press

**Purpose:** The barbell press with the back supported, for days when back pain is 2 or more.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical push.

**Targets:** Shoulders and triceps.

**How:** Set the bench upright inside the rack and the bar on the hooks at about chin height while seated. Sit with your back against the bench and feet flat. Unrack with a grip just outside the shoulders, press straight up, and lower to about chin height under control. Rack it when done.

**Feel:** Shoulders and triceps.

**Avoid:** Arching off the bench, pressing forward, lowering into the face.

**Pain-aware options:** The app shows "Reduce the load" when this press is chosen because of back pain. The person may swap to dumbbells instead.

**Equipment:** Barbell, rack, adjustable bench (upright).

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 5.

# Carries

## Two-Hand Overhead Carry

**Purpose:** Build shoulder stability and a tall trunk with a load overhead. Time first, then load.

**Movement pattern:** Carry.

**Targets:** Shoulders and upper back; trunk and grip secondarily.

**How:** Hold one dumbbell by both ends and press it overhead until the arms are straight. Keep the ribs down and the biceps near the ears. Walk slowly for the prescribed time, then lower the dumbbell to the shoulders and down. It is the last carry of the session, after the press.

**Feel:** Shoulders, upper back, and the trunk working to stay tall.

**Avoid:** Bent elbows, arching the low back, letting the weight drift forward, walking fast.

**Pain-aware options:** Lighter weight or shorter time. On shoulder pain at 3 or more, swap to Front-Hold Carry.

**Equipment:** One dumbbell.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 20–30 sec.

---

## Waiter Carry

**Purpose:** The harder overhead carry: one arm overhead, which adds a side-to-side challenge. Joins in Chapter VII.

**Movement pattern:** Carry, one side.

**Targets:** Shoulder and upper back of the working arm; obliques and trunk.

**How:** Press one dumbbell overhead with the palm facing in and the arm straight. Keep the ribs down and stand tall without leaning. Walk slowly for the prescribed time, lower the weight to the shoulder, and switch sides.

**Feel:** The working shoulder and the side of the trunk.

**Avoid:** Leaning away from the weight, a bent elbow, the weight drifting forward.

**Pain-aware options:** Lighter weight, shorter time, or the two-hand overhead carry. On shoulder pain at 3 or more, swap to Front-Hold Carry.

**Equipment:** One dumbbell.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 20–30 sec per side.

## Preacher Curl

**Purpose:** A curl with the upper arm supported, so the biceps does the work without body swing.

**Movement pattern:** Elbow flexion.

**Targets:** Biceps.

**How:** Set the bench to an incline. Kneel or stand behind it and rest the back of one upper arm on the backrest, armpit near the top edge. Hold a dumbbell palm up. Curl it up, then lower slowly until the arm is nearly straight. Finish one side, then switch.

**Feel:** Front of the upper arm.

**Avoid:** Dropping fast into a fully straight arm, lifting the elbow off the pad, heavy weight.

**Pain-aware options:** Lighter weight or a shorter range at the bottom.

**Equipment:** Adjustable bench (incline), dumbbell.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 10–12 per side.

# Upper Back and Trunk

## Pallof Press

**Purpose:** Train the trunk to resist twisting.

**Movement pattern:** Anti-rotation.

**Targets:** Obliques and deep trunk muscles; glutes and shoulders secondarily.

**How:** Loop a band around a rack upright at chest height. Stand side-on to the rack, far enough out that the band pulls. Hold the band with both hands at the middle of your chest. Brace and press your hands straight out in front, hold a breath's length, and bring them back. Don't let the band turn you. Finish one side, then face the other way.

**Feel:** The side of the trunk nearer the rack, and the whole midsection.

**Avoid:** Rotating toward the rack, leaning away, shrugging, holding the breath through the set.

**Pain-aware options:** Stand closer to the rack for less pull, or use Plank Shoulder Taps.

**Equipment:** Band, rack upright (chest-height anchor).

**Images:** Yes. Generated image sequence.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 8 per side.

---

## Plank Shoulder Taps

**Purpose:** Anti-rotation trunk work with no anchor. The Pallof press fallback.

**Movement pattern:** Anti-rotation.

**Targets:** Trunk and shoulder stabilizers.

**How:** Hold a high plank with hands under the shoulders and feet a little wider than the hips. Lift one hand and tap the opposite shoulder, then put it down. Alternate slowly, keeping the hips still.

**Feel:** Trunk and shoulders.

**Avoid:** Hips swaying side to side, rushing, sagging.

**Pain-aware options:** Widen the feet, or do it with hands on the bench.

**Equipment:** None.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 8 per side.

---

## Band Pull-Apart

**Purpose:** Light upper-back and rear-shoulder work for posture and healthy shoulders. In the warm-up from Chapter II.

**Movement pattern:** Horizontal pull.

**Targets:** Rear delts and upper back.

**How:** Hold a light band in front of your chest with straight arms, hands about shoulder width. Pull the band apart by moving your hands out to the sides until it touches your chest, then return slowly.

**Feel:** Back of the shoulders and between the shoulder blades.

**Avoid:** Shrugging, arching the back, snapping the band back.

**Pain-aware options:** A lighter band or a wider grip.

**Equipment:** Band.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 12.

---

## Band Face Pull

**Purpose:** Upper-back and rear-shoulder work with the arms raised.

**Movement pattern:** Horizontal pull.

**Targets:** Rear delts, upper back, and the muscles that rotate the shoulder outward.

**How:** Anchor a band on the pull-up bar or a rack upright at about face height. Hold it with both hands, palms down, and step back until it pulls. Pull the band toward your face, splitting the hands so they finish beside your ears with the elbows high. Return slowly.

**Feel:** Back of the shoulders and upper back.

**Avoid:** Leaning back, shrugging, pulling with the low back.

**Pain-aware options:** A lighter band or a shorter range.

**Equipment:** Band, pull-up bar or rack upright.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 12.

---

## Chest-Supported DB Reverse Fly

**Purpose:** The loadable upper-back exercise. The chest support keeps the low back out of it.

**Movement pattern:** Horizontal pull.

**Targets:** Rear delts and upper back.

**How:** Set the bench to an incline and lie face down on it, chest on the pad. Hold a light dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging, palms facing each other. With a slight bend in the elbows, raise the arms out to the sides until they are level with the body, squeezing the shoulder blades together. Lower slowly.

**Feel:** Back of the shoulders and between the shoulder blades.

**Avoid:** Heavy weights, swinging, lifting the chest off the pad, shrugging.

**Pain-aware options:** Lighter weights or a shorter range.

**Equipment:** Adjustable bench (incline), dumbbells.

**Images:** Yes. Generated image sequence.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 10.

# Pulling

## Chin-Up (Band-Assisted)

**Purpose:** Vertical pulling with palms facing you, which brings in more biceps. Forge B's eleventh item from Chapter II.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical pull.

**Targets:** Lats and biceps; upper back, grip and core secondarily.

**How:** Loop the band on the pull-up bar and put a foot or knee in it. Grip the bar with palms facing you, about shoulder width. Start in an active hang, pull until your chin is over the bar if comfortable, and lower slowly. Band help is recorded in pounds like the assisted pull-up.

**Feel:** Lats, biceps, grip.

**Avoid:** Swinging, shrugging, dropping from the top.

**Pain-aware options:** More band help, fewer reps, or a slower negative only.

**Equipment:** Pull-up bar, band.

**Images:** Text only.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 5–8.

## Band-Light Pull-Up and Slow Negatives

**Track step:** 2

**Purpose:** Close the gap to a bodyweight pull-up: light band help for full reps, plus slow lowering without a band.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical pull.

**Targets:** Lats and upper back; biceps, grip and core.

**How:** Do the work sets as assisted pull-ups with the lightest band that still lets you finish clean reps. Then do slow negatives: step up on a box until your chin is over the bar, take your feet off, and lower yourself as slowly as you can to an active hang, about 3–5 seconds. Step down and reset between reps.

**Feel:** Lats, upper back, grip.

**Avoid:** Dropping fast in the negative, swinging, jumping into the top position.

**Pain-aware options:** More band help, or fewer negatives.

**Equipment:** Pull-up bar, band, box or bench to step up.

**Images:** Text only.

**Earliest chapter (draft):** II.

**Passed when (draft):** 3 × 5 pull-ups with 10 lb of band help or less, and 3 slow negatives of at least 5 seconds each.

**Custom Forge default:** 3 × 5 band-light, then 3 slow negatives.

## Chest-to-Bar Pull-Up

**Track step:** 4

**Purpose:** An explosive pull-up that brings the chest to the bar, the strength a muscle-up needs.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical pull.

**Targets:** Lats, upper back, biceps.

**How:** From an active hang, pull hard and fast so your chest, around the collarbone, reaches the bar. Lower under control. Each rep starts from a still hang.

**Feel:** Lats and upper back working hard at the top.

**Avoid:** Kipping or swinging into the bar, losing control on the way down.

**Pain-aware options:** Strict pull-ups to chin height instead.

**Equipment:** Pull-up bar.

**Images:** Text only.

**Earliest chapter (draft):** V.

**Passed when (draft):** 8 strict pull-ups in one set, and 3 × 3 pull-ups with the chest touching the bar.

**Custom Forge default:** 3 × 3.

## Straight-Bar Dip and Transition Drills

**Track step:** 5

**Purpose:** Build the pressing strength above the bar and the wrist-and-elbow turn that link a pull-up to a muscle-up.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical push (dip) and pull-to-push transition.

**Targets:** Triceps, chest and shoulders (dip); lats, forearms and shoulders (transition).

**How:** Straight-bar dip: set a barbell in the rack at about hip height, jump or press up to hold yourself over it with straight arms, lower until the bar is near the bottom of your chest, and press back up. Transition drill: with the bar low enough to keep your feet on the floor, grip it with a false grip (wrists over the bar), lean back, then pull your chest to the bar and turn your elbows forward so you finish on top in the dip position. Let your feet help as much as needed.

**Feel:** Triceps and chest in the dip; lats, forearms and shoulders in the turn.

**Avoid:** Diving the shoulders far forward in the dip, a range that pinches the front of the shoulder, snapping through the turn.

**Pain-aware options:** Shorter dip range, more foot help in the transition. Shoulder pain at 3 or more swaps this step (see Readiness gates).

**Equipment:** Barbell and rack (draft: bar on the hooks or safeties at about hip height).

**Images:** Straight-bar dip text only; transition drill gets a generated image sequence.

**Earliest chapter (draft):** VI.

**Passed when (draft):** 3 × 6 straight-bar dips and 3 × 5 transition drills with light foot help.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 5 dips, 2 × 5 transitions.

## Band-Assisted Muscle-Up

**Track step:** 6

**Purpose:** Practice the whole muscle-up with the band carrying part of the load.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical pull to vertical push.

**Targets:** Lats, chest, triceps, shoulders; grip and core.

**How:** Loop a band over the pull-up bar and put your knees or feet in it. Take a false grip. Pull hard toward your lower chest, turn the elbows forward over the bar, and press out to straight arms on top. Lower the way you came, under control.

**Feel:** Lats on the pull, chest and triceps on the press.

**Avoid:** Kipping beyond a small hip drive, one arm turning over before the other, crashing down.

**Pain-aware options:** A stronger band or fewer reps. Shoulder pain at 3 or more swaps this step.

**Equipment:** Pull-up bar with clearance above it, band.

**Images:** Yes. Generated image sequence.

**Earliest chapter (draft):** VII.

**Passed when (draft):** 3 × 2 clean band-assisted muscle-ups with both arms turning over together.

**Custom Forge default:** 3 × 2.

## Wall Handstand Push-Up Negative

**Track step:** 4

**Purpose:** Build the handstand push-up from the top down.

**Movement pattern:** Vertical push (bodyweight, inverted).

**Targets:** Shoulders and triceps; upper back and trunk.

**How:** Fold a towel or mat at the foot of the wall. Kick up to a handstand facing away from the wall, hands about a hand's length from it, or walk up chest-to-wall. Lower slowly, over 3–5 seconds, until the top of your head touches the pad. Come down from the wall and reset; do not press up.

**Feel:** Shoulders and triceps.

**Avoid:** Dropping fast, landing on the head with weight, arching the low back hard.

**Pain-aware options:** A shorter lowering, or feet-elevated pike push-ups. Neck or shoulder pain at 2 or more swaps this.

**Equipment:** Clear wall space, a folded towel or mat.

**Images:** Yes. Generated image sequence.

**Custom Forge default:** 3 × 2–3.

## Kick-Up to Wall

**Track step:** 4

**Purpose:** Learn to kick into a handstand, back to the wall, and come down safely.

**Movement pattern:** Inverted entry.

**Targets:** Shoulders and trunk; hips.

**How:** Stand facing the wall a little more than a body length away. Put your hands down about a hand's length from it and kick one leg up, then the other, so your heels rest on the wall. Hold briefly. To come down, step one foot down at a time; to bail, turn one hand and cartwheel out.

**Feel:** Shoulders and trunk.

**Avoid:** Kicking hard enough to slam the wall, hands too far from the wall, not practising the bail first.

**Pain-aware options:** Chest-to-wall hold instead.

**Equipment:** Clear wall space.

**Images:** Yes. Generated image sequence.

**Earliest chapter (draft):** VI.

**Passed when (draft):** 5 controlled kick-ups each followed by a 15-second hold, plus the bail practised on both sides.

**Custom Forge default:** 3 × 3 kick-ups.

# Balance (Chapter V On)

Forge B's tenth slot from Chapter V, when it is not the glute bridge. Single-Leg Balance and its harder versions are above.

## Lateral Step-Down

**Purpose:** Knee and hip control for downhill and uneven ground, stepping down to the side.

**Movement pattern:** Single leg.

**Targets:** Quads and outer hip of the standing leg.

**How:** Stand side-on on a low step. Slowly bend the standing leg and lower the outside heel toward the floor over about 3 seconds, keeping the knee in line with the toes. Touch lightly and stand back up. Finish one side, then switch.

**Feel:** Standing leg's quad and outer hip.

**Avoid:** Knee caving inward, hip dropping, landing weight on the lowering foot.

**Pain-aware options:** A lower step, a hand on the rack.

**Equipment:** Low step or box.

**Images:** Yes. Generated image sequence.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 5 per side.

## Single-Leg Reach

**Purpose:** Balance and hip control while reaching the free foot in three directions.

**Movement pattern:** Single leg.

**Targets:** Glutes, outer hip, ankle; trunk.

**How:** Stand on one leg with a soft knee. Reach the free foot forward and tap lightly, return, reach to the side, return, then reach back. That is one rep. Bend the standing knee as needed. Switch legs.

**Feel:** Standing leg's glute, outer hip and ankle.

**Avoid:** Putting weight on the reaching foot, knee caving in.

**Pain-aware options:** Shorter reaches, a hand on the rack.

**Equipment:** None.

**Images:** Yes. Generated image sequence.

## Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (Light)

**Purpose:** A light single-leg hinge for hip control and balance. Gated by back pain like the Elevated Light Hinge.

**Movement pattern:** Hinge, single leg.

**Targets:** Glutes and hamstrings of the standing leg; trunk.

**How:** Hold a light dumbbell in the hand opposite the standing leg. With a soft knee, push the hips back and let the free leg rise behind you as the chest lowers, keeping the back flat. Go only as far as the hamstring allows, then stand by driving the hips forward. Switch sides.

**Feel:** Back of the standing leg and glute.

**Avoid:** Rounding the back, twisting the hips open, heavy weight.

**Stop/substitute:** If lower-back symptoms increase, as with the Elevated Light Hinge. Skip it on a day the light hinge would be skipped.

**Equipment:** One light dumbbell; a wall or rack for balance if needed.

**Images:** Yes. Generated image sequence.

**Custom Forge default:** 2 × 6 per side.
