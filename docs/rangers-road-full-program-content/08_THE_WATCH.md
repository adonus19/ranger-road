# Leadership Skill — Stability
Lower voice -> slow movement -> clarify need -> handle next thing -> communicate plan.

# Situational Awareness
Practice noticing:
- exits
- people
- terrain
- weather
- lighting
- hazards
- family location

This is awareness, not paranoia.

# Watch Trial
1. Complete 6-mile hike/walk.
2. Record distance, time, elevation, RPE, symptoms, weather.
3. During route record three landmarks, one weather observation, one hazard, and one route decision.
4. Complete 10 min total loaded carrying in manageable intervals.
5. Complete one practical skill: first aid, navigation, knot, gear management, or tool skill.
6. At 60–120 min record Post-Mission Function:
   - energy
   - patience
   - soreness
   - ability to help at home
