# Chapter I — The Muster
## Weeks 1–4
**Theme:** Answer the call.  
**Trial:** The Gate Trial.  
**Purpose:** Re-establish consistency, movement, aerobic base, faith rhythm, and personal responsibility.

The app schedules these as four full Monday–Sunday weeks. If Day 1 falls Tuesday–Sunday, the days through that first Sunday are a short lead-in using Week 1's matching weekday orders; the full Week 1 begins the following Monday. No additional exercises or progression are added during the lead-in.

# Standard Forge A

Warm-up:
- easy walk — 2 min
- cat-camel — 6
- glute bridge — 10
- bird dog — 5/side
- bodyweight box squat — 8
- wall slide — 8

Work:
- Box Squat — 3 x 6, RPE ~6
- Bench Press — 3 x 6, several reps in reserve
- Assisted Pull-Up — 3 x 5–8
- Supported Split Squat — 2 x 6/leg
- Suitcase Carry — 3 x 30 sec/side
- Bird Dog — 2 x 6/side
- Side Plank — 2 x 15–30 sec/side
- Optional easy walk — 5 min

# Standard Forge B

Warm-up: same.

Work:
- Goblet Squat to Box — 3 x 8
- Step-Up — 3 x 6/leg
- One-Arm DB Row — 3 x 8/side
- Push-Up — 3 x 8–12, never to failure
- Glute Bridge — 3 x 10
- Farmer Carry — 3 x 30–45 sec
- Bird Dog — 2 x 6/side

# Restoration
Use Wednesday and Friday or as needed:
- cat-camel — 6
- bird dog — 5/side
- glute bridge — 10
- half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch — 30 sec/side
- supported squat hold — 20–30 sec
- wall slide — 8
- open-book rotation — 5/side
- easy walk — 1 min

# The Gate Trial

## Part I — Road
2-mile brisk walk.

Record:
- time
- average HR if available
- RPE
- knee response
- back response
- recovery after 5 minutes

## Part II — Gate Circuit
After 5–10 min rest, complete 3 controlled rounds:
- 10 box squats
- 8 push-ups
- 5 assisted pull-ups
- 8 step-ups/leg
- suitcase carry — 30 sec/side
- side plank — 20 sec/side

Record completion time, but do not race.

## Part III — Mind
Write:
- one thing learned about your body
- one thing learned about your character
- one thing learned about your family

## Part IV — Spirit
Read Psalm 121. Spend 10–15 quiet minutes in prayer. Answer:
**What kind of husband and father am I trying to become?**

## Part V — Oath
Write a short personal Ranger's Oath based on strength for service, keeping your word, stewardship, wisdom, faith, and family leadership.
