## Campaign Principles

- Capability over appearance.
- Consistency over heroics.
- Never miss twice.
- No punishment workouts for missed sessions. A missed Forge session may be moved (never added to), as written in `docs/RANGERS_ROAD_PROGRAM.md` under Missed Forge Make-ups.
- Heavy deadlifting is not part of the default program.
- Running is not required during the early campaign.
- Rucking begins conservatively.
- Strength is built for usefulness.
- Faith is practiced and reflected upon, not converted into XP.
- Family leadership is demonstrated through service, presence, responsibility, and follow-through.
- The program should bend when readiness or pain requires it.

## Standard Weekly Rhythm

Most chapters follow:

- Monday: Forge A
- Tuesday: Road / conditioning
- Wednesday: Restoration, skill, or recovery
- Thursday: Forge B
- Friday: short conditioning / restoration
- Saturday: quest, long road, skill integration, or trial
- Sunday: rest, worship, family, and weekly reflection

## Readiness Before Strength or Major Conditioning

Record:

- Sleep hours
- Energy: 1–5
- Back pain: 0–10
- Shoulder pain: 0–10
- Neck pain: 0–10

### Green
Energy 3–5 and pain 0–2:
perform planned mission.

### Yellow
Poor sleep, energy 2, or pain 3–4:
reduce volume approximately 25%; do not progress load.

### Red
Major symptom increase, illness, new radiating pain, weakness/numbness, or other concerning symptoms:
skip strength work; use easy movement/restoration only if appropriate and seek medical evaluation when warranted.

## Morning Watch

5–10 minutes:

1. Read assigned Scripture reference.
2. Brief prayer.
3. Review today's orders.
4. Ask: **What does my family need from me today?**

## Evening Watch

3–5 minutes:

- Win
- Failure / missed standard
- Gratitude
- Tomorrow

## Daily Nutrition / Recovery Baseline

- Protein at breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
- Fruit or vegetable at least three times daily.
- Reduce sweet tea gradually.
- Water before lunch and dinner.
- Minimum three alcohol-free evenings per week.
- Stop eating at comfortable satisfaction.
- Aim toward 7+ hours of sleep.
