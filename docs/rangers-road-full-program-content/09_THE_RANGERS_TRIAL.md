# The Ranger's Trial

## Phase I — The Long Road
Complete a 6–8 mile trail or mixed-terrain route of moderate difficulty at sustainable effort.

## Phase II — Bearing the Load
Accumulate 20–30 minutes total of light ruck/carry work in manageable segments. No heavy death march.

## Phase III — Strength Demonstration
After appropriate recovery:
- 15 clean push-ups
- pull-up progression test
- 10 controlled squats
- 10 step-ups/leg
- farmer carry — 60 sec
- suitcase carry — 60 sec/side
- side plank — 30–45 sec/side

The purpose is usable strength after an endurance effort, not maximal exhaustion.

## Phase IV — Fieldcraft
Complete:
1. navigation task
2. first-aid scenario
3. practical skill task (knot, gear, tool, route planning, etc.)

## Phase V — Mind
Spend 20 minutes without phone/audio. Review:
- what changed
- what failed
- what surprised you
- what you can now do
- what still needs work

## Phase VI — Faith
Read your Month 1 answer to:
**What kind of husband and father am I trying to become?**

Read Psalm 121 and 2 Timothy 4:7. Pray.

Answer:
**What kind of husband and father am I becoming now?**

Preserve both entries.

## Phase VII — Hearth
Return home and remain present. Later record:
- energy
- patience
- soreness
- ability to help
- ability to engage with spouse/children

The trial is not truly complete if the physical challenge leaves you incapable of fulfilling ordinary family responsibilities.
