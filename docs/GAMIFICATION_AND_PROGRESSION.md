# Gamification and Progression

## Principle

Use theme and progression without cheap gamification.

Do not:
- use shame streaks
- award Faith XP
- award Hearth XP
- convert prayer into points
- equate opening the app with progress
- encourage unsafe overtraining to preserve streaks

## Use

- chapters
- trials
- capability goals
- milestone history
- mission completion
- visual campaign map
- personal records
- before/after comparisons
- trial journal entries
- optional photos

## Attributes

Measured:
- Might
- Endurance
- Movement
- Resolve

Non-numeric:
- Faith
- Hearth
- Wisdom
- Fieldcraft competency milestones

## Capability Goals

Example:
**Carry a Child — One Mile**

Contributors:
- walking endurance
- carry duration
- grip
- trunk stability
- leg strength

Milestones:
- 30 lb x 1 min
- 30 lb x 3 min
- 30–40 lb x 5 min
- carry intervals
- 1/4 mile
- 1/2 mile
- 1 mile with planned switches/rest

## Missed Mission UX

Offer:
- Full Mission
- Reduced Mission
- Restoration Only
- Rest — record reason

Weekly summary:
- planned
- completed
- reduced
- abandoned

No guilt copy. A missed Forge session is offered a calm make-up the next morning (see Missed Forge Make-ups in `RANGERS_ROAD_PROGRAM.md`); it is never shown as a debt or a count.
